Android Emulator Online

Run any Android app in your browser.

Upload an APK and you get a real Android phone to tap, swipe and type on. Share the link, and everyone who opens it gets a fresh phone with your app installed.

Your apps

    Every session runs on its own Pixel 7 with Android 15, booted from a clean state. When the session ends the phone is destroyed along with everything on it. Apps you upload are only listed in this browser; anyone you send a link to can open that app.
